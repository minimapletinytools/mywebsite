Perfect Woman is a "Perfect Woman Life Simulator". In the game you choose to be one of several different characters at each stage of your life. Progress through the game by matching the required poses for each character. The difficulties of these poses are dependent on the choices you've made in the past. There are no right or wrong choices. The game ends when you die. At death, you can see a summary of your life before you start the game over.

Perfect Woman touches on the power of games as a medium for artistic expression. It provides the framework in the way rules for the player to construct their own 'Perfect Woman' narrative. But it also expresses on a level beyond its content. While the content of the game and its myriad characters have meaning unto itself, another layer of cultural critique is present when considering the narrative framework itself. 

This game is a collaboration with the talented German Animator, Artist, and Game Designer, Lea Schoenfelder. For more information please visit [our website](http://www.perfectwomangame.com).
