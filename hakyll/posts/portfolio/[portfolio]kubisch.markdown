Cube 3 is an innovative 3 dimensional twist on tic-tac-toe. Do not be deceived by its slick and simple controls. You'll find this game to be much more challenging than its 9 piece predecessor. Play against the computer or your peers. 

Tilt to look around, tap to select and swipe to rotate and end your turn. Connect 3 in a row to win. 

Now availabe for [Android](https://market.android.com/details?id=cube3.robotbear.org), ~~iPhone and iPad~~!
